# 11. No flashcards; spaced review stays in the book

**English** | [Português](0011-no-flashcards.pt-BR.md)

**Status:** accepted · **Source:** `plano/PLANO.md §2, §19`

## Context

Flashcard apps and card files would mean another format to build, test and keep in sync with the book.

## Decision

Syllabook makes no flashcards. Spaced review is part of each book: quick-review questions about earlier chapters, cheat sheets and a final mock exam. Generated audio and video are out of scope too.

## Consequences

Everything the reader needs to review is inside the book, in every format (Markdown, website, EPUB, Notion).
