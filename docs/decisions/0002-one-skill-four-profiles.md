# 2. One skill with four area profiles

**English** | [Português](0002-one-skill-four-profiles.pt-BR.md)

**Status:** accepted · **Source:** `plano/PLANO.md §2, §6`

## Context

Programming, languages, music and history are taught in very different ways, but most of the work (syllabus, research, checks, publishing) is the same.

## Decision

A single skill, with four teaching profiles read on demand: `stem`, `languages`, `skills` and `knowledge` (at most two per book).

## Consequences

One installation covers any topic, and each book reads only its own profile. A topic that fits no profile gets a method of its own, explained in the assessment.
